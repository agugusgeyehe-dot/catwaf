The Settings page covers appearance and a couple of housekeeping items — it doesn't touch WAF behavior at all (that all lives under WAF Engine and Protection in the sidebar).

**Themes** — four to choose from: Pure Black (default, OLED-friendly), Dark, Pure White, and Midnight. Switching applies instantly and is remembered for your next visit.

**Welcome Tour** — the guided walkthrough shown the first time you log in. "Replay Tour" brings it back anytime, useful if you want a refresher or you're showing someone else around.

**About CatWAF** — version and runtime info, pulled live from the server rather than hardcoded, so it always matches what's actually running. See "About CatWAF and version info" for what those fields mean.
